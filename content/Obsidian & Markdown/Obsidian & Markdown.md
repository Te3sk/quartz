# Obsidian & Markdown – Introduction
This section provides an overview of **Markdown** and **Obsidian**, two essential tools for managing structured, portable, and collaborative documentation.  
Markdown gives you a lightweight syntax to create clear and readable documents, while Obsidian extends this with features like graph view, backlinks, and internal linking that make it easier to build and maintain a knowledge base.  
Together, they provide the foundation for our company’s Tech Vault: simple to write, easy to share, and powerful enough to grow with our needs.
